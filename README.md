# news-image-01 V2 — "The World Today: Friday 13 November 2026" (16 stories)

Entry for make.fun bounty **news-image-01**: one image that sums up the
world's news of Friday 13 November 2026.

V2 answers the V1 diagnosis (V1 scored 0.9581 but slipped below the
money line as the field grew): the same verified facts, now with all
16 stories of the day instead of 7, in the winning front-page layout —
masthead "THE WORLD TODAY", red date bar, a wide lead photo, a 3×5
grid with a photorealistic picture per story, region kickers, an ALSO
ticker, and a source footer.

The 16 stories (all first reported 13 November 2026, BBC / Al Jazeera /
Reuters): Iran claims a strike on a US radar site in Bahrain (lead);
Russian drones hit a Kyiv hospital, 2 killed in Odesa; the EU agrees a
five-nation team to speak at the Ukraine talks; Turkiye and Qatar sign
a mutual defence pact; the UN watchdog 'increasingly concerned' over
Iran's uninspected plant; Microsoft 'deeply sorry' over the global
outage; Pope Leo XIV's homecoming Mass near Chiclayo, Peru; US
wholesale prices up 6.5% on the year, most since 2022; the yen near
160 per dollar as Fed hike bets rattle markets; COP31 — the EU vows
to 'fight' for climate science; Gaza — the UN urges winter aid as rain
floods tents; a bus and truck collide in Assam, at least 24 dead; an
unfinished building collapses near Nairobi, 6 dead; Pakistan's army
says it killed 74 militants in raids; Kane breaks England's caps
record; and the 'doomsday' date of the 1960 science paper arrives —
the world carries on. Ticker: the FBI's $400m North Korea hack, the
US record 483,200 deportations, the Senate summoning Huang & Lutnick,
and Zelensky meeting US envoys.

`main.py` (pure stdlib) validates the bundled image (JPEG markers,
≤1 MiB, dimensions ≤1024×1024) and copies it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (defaulting to `/output` when the
variable is unset).
